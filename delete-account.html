Delete Your Account

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your MedBuddy account and all your health data.

Important Note:
Account deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, all your glucose readings, health trends, profile information, and doctor details will be wiped from our servers and cannot be recovered.

How to delete your account

  • Open the MedBuddy App
    Ensure you are logged in to the account you wish to delete.
  • Navigate to Profile
    Tap on the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  • Find Account Settings
    Scroll down to the Settings section at the bottom of the Profile screen.
  • Tap Delete Account
    Select the Delete Account option (highlighted in red).
  • Confirm Deletion
    A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap Delete to permanently remove your data.

What data is deleted?

  • Authentication: Your Google Sign-In linked account.
  • Health Data: All logged blood glucose readings and meal period notes.
  • Profile Info: Your name, age, weight, and blood type records.
  • Doctor Info: Hospital names, doctor specialties, and appointment dates.
  • Media: Your uploaded profile picture.
