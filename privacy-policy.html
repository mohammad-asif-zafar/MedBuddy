Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

MedBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application and related services.

Your Data, Your Control: MedBuddy is designed with privacy at its core. We use industry-standard encryption to ensure your sensitive medical data remains private and secure.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with the best glucose management experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identity: Name, email address, and profile picture provided through Google Sign-In.
  • Diabetes Metrics: Blood glucose levels, meal-specific timing (Before/After Breakfast, etc.), and carbohydrate intake.
  • Vital Health Stats: Blood pressure readings, weight logs, BMI calculations, and exercise/step data.
  • Medical Records: Lab result values (HbA1c, Cholesterol, etc.) extracted via manual entry or OCR scanning of your reports.
  • Care Network: Contact details for your primary care physician and caregivers you choose to share data with.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to power the following features:

  • Smart Dashboards: Generating real-time trends and Cubic Bezier charts of your health history.
  • AI Insights: Analyzing your data to provide personalized tips, such as identifying meal-time spikes.
  • Emergency Alerts: Triggering notifications for critically high or low glucose levels.
  • Family Sharing: Enabling your authorized family members to monitor your logs for your safety.
  • Clinical Reporting: Generating professional PDF summaries for you to share with your doctor.

3. Data Storage & Security

We utilize Google Firebase for secure data handling:

  • Firestore: Your health records are stored in a secure cloud database, synchronized in real-time across your devices.
  • Authentication: We use Google Firebase Auth to manage secure access to your account.
  • Storage: Medical PDF reports and profile photos are stored in encrypted cloud buckets.

4. Your Privacy Choices

You have full control over your data within the app settings:

  • Data Portability: You can export your full health history into PDF or CSV formats at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: You may delete specific logs or your entire account and associated data directly from the profile settings.
  • Sharing Control: You can revoke access to family members or caregivers instantly through the Family Care dashboard.
