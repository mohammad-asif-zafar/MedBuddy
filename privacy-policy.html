Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

MedBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your information when you use the MedBuddy mobile application.

Your Privacy Matters

MedBuddy securely stores your glucose records using Google Firebase. Your information is transmitted using encrypted connections and is accessible only after signing in with your Google account.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to provide MedBuddy's glucose tracking features.

  • Google Account Information
    Your name, email address and profile picture when you sign in using Google Sign-In.
  • Blood Glucose Records
    Blood glucose readings, reading date, reading time, meal period (such as Before Breakfast or After Dinner), and optional notes entered by you.
  • Diagnostic Information
    Basic crash logs and performance information that help us improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to provide MedBuddy's core features.

  • Securely authenticate your account using Google Sign-In.
  • Store and synchronize your glucose records across your devices.
  • Display glucose history, trends and charts.
  • Generate PDF reports that you choose to export or share.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability and performance of the app.

3. Data Storage & Security

We use Google Firebase services to securely store your data.

  • Firebase Authentication is used to securely sign you into your account.
  • Cloud Firestore stores your glucose records securely in the cloud so they remain available on your signed-in devices.
  • Data is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

4. Your Choices

  • You may edit or delete your glucose records at any time.
  • You may sign out of your Google account whenever you wish.
  • You may stop using the application at any time.
  • If you would like your account and associated data permanently removed, please contact us using the email address below.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Your data is only shared with Google Firebase services that are required to operate the application securely.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
