Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 20, 2026

MedBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture (via Google Sign-In).
  • Health Data: Blood glucose readings, meal periods, notes, and medical metrics (age, weight, blood type).
  • Medical Information: Doctor names, specialties, hospitals, and appointment dates.

2. Use of Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the MedBuddy services.
  • Track your health trends and provide dashboard visualizations.
  • Send you push notifications for log reminders and health alerts via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Personalize your experience with custom greetings and theme preferences.

3. Data Storage and Security

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Storage) to securely store and sync your data across devices. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no security system is impenetrable.

4. Third-Party Services

MedBuddy utilizes Google Play Services and Firebase for its core functionality. These services may collect information used to identify you.

5. Your Rights

You can view, update, or delete your health records and profile information at any time within the application. You can sign out to stop syncing data to the cloud.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@medbuddy.com.
